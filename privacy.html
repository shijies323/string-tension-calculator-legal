English

Core features in this version run locally. The app does not automatically upload instrument presets, favorites or comparisons to the developer.

1. Operator and scope

This policy is provided by Shijie Sun for the current version of String Tension. Contact: amplymusicstudio@gmail.com. It covers local processing within the app and information handling when you choose to contact the developer.

2. Information processed locally

To provide the features you select, the app stores instrument preset names and types, lengths, tuning, reference pitch, favorites, saved comparisons, unfinished comparison inputs, and language and appearance preferences on your device. These restore your settings and calculations and are not automatically sent to developer servers. System language and appearance information is used for the corresponding interface features.

This version requires no account and integrates no advertising, cross-app tracking, behavioral analytics or crash-reporting service that sends data to the developer. Core features do not request contacts, location, camera or microphone permissions.

3. Exports, sharing and external links

When you choose to export, the app generates a PDF or image on your device and opens system sharing. Exports contain selected strings, calculation inputs, results and names you choose to display. You choose recipients, storage and transmission; destination apps or services handle files under their own policies. The app does not automatically send exports to the developer.

Opening a manufacturer source, platform license or other external link causes your browser or destination app to visit that website. The website may process request data such as your IP address and browser information under its own policy. The app does not automatically append local instrument presets or comparisons to these links.

The online policy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit the online version, GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes under the GitHub Privacy Statement (https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Reading the offline policy within the app does not itself contact this hosting service.

4. Information you send when contacting us

If you email the developer, we receive your email address, message and attachments only to respond, investigate your issue and meet applicable legal obligations. Provide only what is needed. Processing is based on providing support you request where contractually necessary, legitimate support interests where recognized by applicable law, or legal obligations. You may withdraw consent where consent is the applicable basis.

The contact mailbox uses Gmail. When you choose to send email, your email provider and Gmail process message content and technical information under their policies and may use servers in other countries or regions. The current app has no backend that automatically uploads personal records, and we do not sell your information or use support messages for advertising profiles.

5. Retention, backups and deletion

Use My library to delete presets, remove favorites and delete saved comparisons. Temporary export files may remain in app caches managed by your operating system’s cache or app-data controls. Delete separately saved or shared files at their destinations. Device or browser cleanup, uninstalling, backups and migration depend on your system and settings; system backups are not app-provided cloud synchronization.

Support messages are retained only as long as needed to answer, handle related disputes or meet legal obligations. You may request deletion of messages no longer needed. The developer cannot remotely read or delete records stored only on your device.

6. Security and platform services

Personal records use local app storage protected by operating-system app isolation. The app adds no separate password or end-to-end encryption to these records. Protect device access and backup accounts. Encryption of the offline string-catalog resource does not provide password protection for personal presets or exports.

React Native, Expo and local-storage components support the interface, local calculations, data access and exports. App stores, operating systems and backup, email or sharing services you select may process download, purchase, diagnostic, backup or transfer information under their settings and policies.

7. Your rights and minors

For information you send to the developer, contact the email address to exercise applicable rights of access, correction, deletion, copies, restriction, objection, portability and withdrawal of consent. You may also complain to a competent regulator. Manage local records directly through app and device controls. We request only information needed to verify and handle a request.

The app is for general instrument users and is not designed specifically for children. A guardian should help minors understand the information and privacy settings. If we learn that we received a minor’s personal information without legally required guardian consent, we will verify and handle or delete it as required.

8. Policy updates and contact

Policy updates show a version date. Material changes to personal-data handling will be highlighted, with renewed consent obtained where legally required. Send privacy questions or rights requests to Shijie Sun at amplymusicstudio@gmail.com.

中文版

当前版本的核心功能在本地运行。乐器预设、收藏和对比方案不会由本应用自动上传给开发者。

1. 运营者与适用范围

本政策由 Shijie Sun 制定，适用于“琴弦张力”（String Tension）当前版本。联系邮箱：amplymusicstudio@gmail.com。本政策说明应用功能中的本地处理，以及您主动联系开发者时的信息处理。

2. 本地处理哪些资料

为提供您选择的功能，应用会在设备本地保存乐器预设名称、乐器类型、弦长、调音、基准音高、琴弦收藏、对比方案、尚未完成的对比输入，以及语言和外观偏好。这些资料用于恢复您的设置和计算内容，不会由本应用自动发送到开发者服务器。系统语言和外观信息仅用于相应界面功能。

本版本无需注册账号，未接入广告、跨应用跟踪、行为统计或向开发者发送数据的崩溃上报服务，也不会为核心功能索取通讯录、定位、相机或麦克风权限。

3. 导出、分享与外部链接

您主动导出对比结果时，应用会在设备上生成 PDF 或图片，并调用系统分享功能。导出的内容包括所选琴弦、计算条件、结果，以及您选择显示的名称等信息。接收方、存储位置和发送行为由您的选择决定，目标应用或服务依其自己的政策处理文件；本应用不会自动把导出文件发给开发者。

点击厂家资料、平台许可等外部链接后，浏览器或目标应用会访问相应网站。网站可能处理访问所需的 IP 地址、浏览器信息等请求数据，具体以其政策为准。本应用不会在这些链接中自动附加您的本地乐器预设或对比方案。

本政策的在线网页由 GitHub Pages 托管。访问在线版本时，GitHub 会为安全目的记录访客的 IP 地址，具体处理见 GitHub 隐私声明（https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement）。在应用内阅读离线政策本身不会访问该托管服务。

4. 您主动联系时的信息

如果您通过邮箱联系开发者，我们会收到您发送的邮箱地址、邮件正文及附件，仅用于答复、排查您提出的问题和履行相关法定义务。请只提供处理问题所需的信息。此类处理依据提供您请求的支持服务所必需的条件、适用法律认可的支持服务正当利益或相关法定义务进行；以同意为依据的处理，您可依法撤回同意。

联系邮箱使用 Gmail。您选择发送邮件时，您的邮件服务商与 Gmail 会依各自政策处理邮件内容和技术信息，可能使用位于其他国家或地区的服务器。当前应用不通过自身后台自动上传个人资料，也不出售您的资料或将反馈内容用于广告画像。

5. 保留、备份与删除

您可在“我的”删除乐器预设、取消收藏和删除对比方案。应用缓存中可能保留导出时生成的临时文件，由操作系统的缓存或应用数据管理功能处理。您另行保存或分享的文件，需要在相应位置删除。设备或浏览器清理、应用卸载、系统备份与迁移的效果取决于您的系统及设置；系统备份并非本应用提供的云同步。

反馈邮件仅保留完成答复、处理相关争议或履行法定义务所必要的时间。您可以请求删除无需继续保留的反馈信息。开发者无法远程读取或删除只保存在您设备上的资料。

6. 安全与平台服务

个人预设等资料使用应用本地存储，并受操作系统应用隔离机制保护；本应用未对这些个人记录额外设置独立密码或端到端加密。请保护设备解锁方式及备份账号。离线琴弦目录的资源加密，不等同于对个人预设或导出文件提供密码保护。

React Native、Expo 和本地存储组件用于界面、本地计算、资料读取与导出功能。应用商店、操作系统以及您选用的备份、邮件或分享服务，可能按其设置处理下载、购买、诊断、备份或文件传输信息；这些平台行为适用相应服务的政策。

7. 您的权利与未成年人

对于您向开发者提供的信息，您可通过联系邮箱依法申请查阅、更正、删除、获取副本或行使适用的限制处理、反对、可携带和撤回同意等权利，也可向有管辖权的监管部门投诉。本地记录可直接通过应用和设备功能管理。我们只会要求核实和处理请求所必需的信息。

本应用面向一般乐器使用者，非专为儿童设计。未成年人使用时，建议由监护人协助理解资料与隐私设置。若我们得知收到需要监护人同意而未取得同意的未成年人个人信息，会依法核实并处理或删除。

8. 政策更新与联系

政策更新会在本页注明版本日期。涉及个人信息处理方式的实质变化，会以显著方式提示，并在法律要求时重新取得同意。隐私问题或权利请求请联系 Shijie Sun：amplymusicstudio@gmail.com。
