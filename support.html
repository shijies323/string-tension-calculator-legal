How do I restore TENSION Pro?

Use the Apple account originally used for the purchase. Open My library, select Unlock TENSION Pro, then Restore purchases. If the purchase is not found, check the account used for App Store purchases and try again with an internet connection. If access is already active, the unlock card is hidden.

Restoring Pro restores access to its features. It does not recover deleted instrument presets, favorites, or comparisons.

Why does a tension value differ from another source?

Check that the string model, vibrating length, tuning, reference pitch, and units match. Manufacturer data may use different reference conditions or rounding. Some catalog entries use estimates, identified in the app alongside their calculation conditions. If a value still looks incorrect, email the details and a link to the source you are comparing.

Can I recover or move my saved records?

Instrument presets, favorites, and comparisons are stored locally. This version has no developer-operated account or cloud sync, and the developer cannot recover records from your device. Device backups and transfers depend on your system settings. Exported images and PDFs are comparison reports, not restorable app backups. Contact us before uninstalling if you are troubleshooting a problem with saved records.

How do I request a refund?

For App Store purchases, visit Apple’s Report a Problem website. Apple handles refund requests under its process and applicable law.

如何恢复 TENSION Pro？

请使用原购买时的 Apple 账号。打开“我的”，选择“解锁 TENSION Pro”，再点击“恢复购买”。如果未找到购买，请核对 App Store 购买所使用的账号，并在联网状态下重试。Pro 权限已生效时，解锁入口会隐藏。

恢复购买会恢复 Pro 功能权限，不会找回已删除的乐器预设、收藏或对比方案。

为什么张力与其他资料不一致？

请先核对琴弦型号、实际振动弦长、调弦、基准音高及单位是否一致。厂家资料可能使用不同的参考条件或舍入方式；部分目录条目使用估算值，应用内会标明数据类别及计算条件。如仍有疑问，请通过邮箱发送相关参数，以及用于比较的资料链接。

能否恢复或迁移已保存的记录？

乐器预设、收藏和对比方案保存在设备本地。当前版本没有开发者运营的账号或云同步服务，开发者无法从您的设备中找回记录。系统备份和设备迁移的效果取决于系统设置。导出的图片和 PDF 是对比报告，不能作为可恢复的应用备份。如正在排查本地记录问题，请先联系支持，再考虑卸载应用。

如何申请退款？

App Store 购买可前往 Apple 报告问题网站申请退款。退款由 Apple 按其流程及适用法律处理。
